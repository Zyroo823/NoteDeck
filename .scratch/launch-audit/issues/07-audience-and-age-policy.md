# Set supported countries and age policy

Status: needs-info
Priority: P1

The operator is based in the Philippines, but the countries NoteDeck will serve and its intended user age range are still undecided. Decide whether the app will support child users, what age-assurance or parent/guardian process is needed, and how to handle privacy requests from guardians. Do not claim age compliance until the policy is reviewed for the intended markets. See `docs/launch-audit-2026-11-16.md`.
